How to play it
Wet switch (A)
Tap: wet channel on/off. Hold: freeze the reverb tail, delay repeats or slip slice. With LATCH on, a hold toggles freeze.
Loop switch (L)
The looper is always listening. Tap: loop what you just played. Hold from off: record a new loop until release. Hold while playing: overdub. Tap while playing: stop.
Clock
The sample rate of both channels, 2–64 kHz. The loop buffer is a fixed number of samples, so a lower clock means longer loops and delays, a darker, grittier sound, and a pitch drop for anything already recorded.
Loop modes
ENV: playing interrupts the loop and the current slice repeats until you stop (LENGTH = slice size, MODIFY = sensitivity). TAPE: LENGTH = loop length, MODIFY = speed and direction. STRETCH: LENGTH = slice size, MODIFY = stretch amount and direction, pitch unchanged.
Routing
What feeds the wet channel: your input, input plus the loop, or only the loop.
Knobs
Drag up/down, scroll, or use arrow keys. Double-click resets.
