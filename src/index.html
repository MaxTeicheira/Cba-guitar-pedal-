Presets

The PRESET toggle between the footswitches recalls them; the middle position is Live. On the pedal: hold one switch 3 s, then also the other 3 s (right first saves Preset 2).

How to play it
Left switch (A): wet channel
Tap: on/off. Hold: freeze (Soup becomes a pad, Relay a looping echo, Flip a repeating chord). With LATCH on, a hold toggles freeze.
Right switch (L): micro-looper
Always listening while off. Tap: play back what you just played. Tap again: back to listening, which replaces the loop as you play. Hold: overdub your clean input.
Both switches
Hold both: the knobs and toggles set the hidden options (green). Tap both three times quickly: true bypass; tap either to leave. Right switch held + MIX: effect volume.
Clock
The sample rate of both channels, in harmonized steps from 2 to 64 kHz (SMOOTH dip: continuous). It sets the loop length: 128 000 samples, 64 000 with HALF.
Wet modes
SOUP: spectral reverb (TIME decay, MODIFY character). RELAY: an exact number of equal repeats (TIME delay, MODIFY repeats; max = infinite). FLIP: harmonies of 4ths, 5ths and octaves (TIME lag between notes, MODIFY harmony).
Loop modes
BURST: up to 8 slices of the loop as a pattern; play loud to scramble it. RADIO: MODIFY tunes five stations (tape, ambient, orchestral, shoegaze, dance), LENGTH changes per station. MASK: loud parts get disguised (LENGTH character, MODIFY threshold; fully down = the pure loop).
Routing
With both channels on: the wet channel processes the input, input + loop, or only the loop. A loop sent through the wet channel is 100% wet unless you raise BLEND.
Knobs
Drag up/down, scroll, or use arrow keys. Double-click resets.
